Advanced manual connection

History imports require a matching signed-in account, checked before and after collection. Missing, unrecognized or different accounts block import. Use your YouTube channel ID (UC…) or @handle and your TikTok user ID or @handle. YouTube handle verification may require opening the account menu on the history page.

When enabled, captures newly rendered messages in conversations you open while signed in. It does not scrape older history, scroll conversations or send messages.
