Connect installed extension

Already installed? Open and unlock Lifenizer, then connect. Need the desktop app?

1. Install the extension

Chrome / Edge / Chromium

  1. Download the Chrome / Edge ZIP above and extract it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome or Chromium, or edge://extensions in Edge.
  3. Enable Developer mode, choose Load unpacked and select the extracted folder.

Firefox

  1. Download the Firefox ZIP above and extract it.
  2. Open about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox.
  3. Choose Load Temporary Add-on and select manifest.json in the extracted folder.

This temporary installation lasts until Firefox restarts. Load it again after restarting.

2. Connect to Lifenizer

  1. Open the desktop app and unlock the vault you want to use.
  2. Choose Connect installed extension above, then Open Lifenizer and connect on the extension page.
  3. Confirm Connect in the desktop app. If your browser asks to access local device apps and services, choose Allow.

Prefer a QR code? In the desktop app, open Imports → Browser extension → Show QR code. In the extension popup choose Connect / Pair with QR, scan the app window or a QR image, then confirm. No port or token copying is needed.

Keep the app open and unlocked while importing. Choose your YouTube or TikTok account, or enable WhatsApp Web or Discord Web capture, in extension Settings.
