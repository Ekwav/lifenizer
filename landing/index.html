Lifenizer

A private searchable archive for the conversations, documents, recordings, chats, browser traces, and transcripts that make up your life.

Open web app Security model

One vault, many sources.

Lifenizer normalizes life data into encrypted conversations with participants, timestamps, artifacts, searchable segments, and relation facts.

Recordings

Live sessions group speech chunks into a single participant-aware conversation.

Documents

Scanned PDFs, files, and OCR outputs live beside the original artifact metadata.

Chats

WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, Discord, and manual text imports share the same model.

Relations

Detected facts become graph edges tied back to their evidence.

Local-first search.

Synced payloads stay encrypted on the server. Devices decrypt locally, rebuild search indexes, and let you search text, people, sources, dates, and relationships.
