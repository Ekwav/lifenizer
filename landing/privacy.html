Archive contents

Synced archive payloads are encrypted before storage. Your device decrypts them for local search.

Service metadata

The service also handles account information, authentication, usage records and operational data needed to run it. These are distinct from encrypted archive contents.

Explicit processing

Importing through hosted services, transcription, OCR and analysis can share selected plaintext with the service and configured processing providers.

Browser capture

Browser history and page content can reveal sensitive information. Use the companion extension’s capture controls and review what you collect.
