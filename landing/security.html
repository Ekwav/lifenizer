SECURITY

Your archive, on your terms.

Synced archive contents are encrypted on your device. Hosted processing has a different boundary.

← Back to Lifenizer

Encrypted sync

The backend stores encrypted archive envelopes scoped to your account and vault. Account, usage and operational metadata are not the same as encrypted archive contents.

Local search

Your device decrypts the archive and builds its search index locally. Protect your devices and vault passphrase, and keep copies of important originals.

Hosted processing

Transcription, OCR and analysis may need the plaintext content you submit. Review the action before sending private material for processing.

Browser companion

The extension can collect browsing history and selected page content when enabled. Review its permissions and capture controls before connecting it.

Early software is still evolving. Read the privacy notes before adding sensitive material.
